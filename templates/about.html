Who We Are

More Than Just a Restaurant

The Lounge is a beloved restaurant and café nestled in the charming St George's Square in Droitwich Spa, Worcestershire. We opened our doors with one simple mission: to serve great food and create a space where everyone feels at home.

Whether you're stopping in for a morning coffee, a leisurely brunch with friends, or an intimate dinner for two — The Lounge is your home away from home. Our warm, relaxed atmosphere and friendly team make every visit a memorable one.

We serve breakfast, brunch, lunch, and dinner throughout the week, with a seasonal menu that celebrates the best of British and Italian cuisine. And if you're planning something special, we're available for private hire too.

A Day at The Lounge

All-day brunch, Italian-inspired plates and a late-night bar — The Lounge shifts with the rhythm of the day.
